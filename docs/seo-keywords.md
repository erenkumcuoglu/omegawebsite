# SEO keyword research: omegadijital.com

Research date: 2026-11-23. Scope: Steam Wallet, PlayStation Store / PSN, PUBG Mobile UC, Razer Gold, Google Play, Apple Gift Card (App Store & iTunes), plus "buy with crypto / USDT" across 10 locales (en, tr, es, pt-BR, fr, de, ru, ar, id, zh-Hans).

## Method and limits

- Sources: live web search result pages (titles and snippets act as a proxy for what ranks), competitor product and category pages (Bitrefill, CoinGate, CoinsBee, dundle, SEAGM, Codashop, Lapakgaming, GamerMarkt, AR-PAY, Buffget, Topuplive, KaBuM!, Turkcell Pasaj, Cimri), official help pages (Valve, Sony, Google, Apple, Razer, Midasbuy), and press coverage.
- **No search volumes.** Demand is marked **high / medium / low** as a relative estimate within the locale. Each rating rests on what kind of competitor targets the term: dedicated category URLs on several big sellers, many 2026 how-to articles, or the term in shop page titles. Treat all demand ratings as estimates. Validate with Google Search Console after launch, or with Google Keyword Planner, which is free with an Ads account.
- Keywords are written the way they appear in competitor URLs, titles and how-to headlines. Include the common no-diacritics variants in Turkish ("cuzdan", "satin al"), because competitor URLs use them (for example gamermarkt.com/tr/urunler/steam-cuzdan-kodu).
- Competition: **Very high** = global marketplaces plus the official brand page rank. **High** = several established regional sellers. **Medium** = mostly blogs and small sellers. **Low** = few targeted pages found.

## 1. Summary: top findings

1. **Region lock is the main informational demand, and it is also the content gap.** Every product here is region- or currency-locked, and buyers search "does X work in country Y" and "X code not working" in every language. Big marketplaces cover this thinly on product pages. A small shop can rank explainers on this topic. Our own checkout also depends on it: a wrong-region code leads to a refund dispute.
2. **The crypto angle is a niche with weak competition outside Bitrefill and CoinGate.** "buy steam gift card with crypto/usdt" is dominated by Bitrefill (hundreds of localized URLs), CoinGate (localized articles in es/fr/de/ru) and CoinsBee. USDT-specific and network-specific terms ("TRC20", "BEP20", "Binance Pay") have far less targeted content. In ar, tr, id and zh the only crypto-specific gift-card content found was from Bitrefill, AR-PAY and V2EX threads. That makes "USDT + product + region" long-tails the most realistic wins.
3. **Turkey:** Steam has used **USD, not TL,** for Turkish accounts since 2023-11-20 (MENA-USD region). "TL Steam kodu" is now an outdated query that still gets asked. Buyers need **USD wallet codes** (US or MENA-USD), so "steam cüzdan kodu dolar" / "steam türkiye dolar kodu" is a high-value page. For PUBG, Turkish sellers split **"TR ID"** and **"Global ID"** UC. That is a key on-page distinction.
4. **Russia:** Demand is massive, but it goes to workarounds: "пополнить стим", "пополнение PSN Турция", "турецкий аккаунт PS". Russian buyers often hold **Turkish or Kazakh** PSN and Steam accounts. Russian bank cards have been blocked on Steam and PSN since 2022, and Google Play billing has been paused in Russia since March 2022. USDT fits this audience well. Competition comes from vc.ru and dtf.ru advertorials, not big brands.
5. **Arabic:** Searchers mostly use the Gulf store regions: "بطاقة ستيم سعودي", "بطاقة بلايستيشن سعودي / اماراتي", "شحن شدات ببجي". PUBG demand ("شدات") is very high. AR-PAY already publishes Arabic Binance Pay USDT guides, so a USDT angle is proven but not saturated.
6. **Indonesian:** The market runs on top-ups, not gift cards: "top up UC PUBG murah", "voucher steam wallet IDR". Codashop, Lapakgaming and VCGamers dominate with local e-wallet payment. Crypto is a niche angle, so rank on "beli voucher steam pakai USDT" rather than head terms.
7. **Chinese (Simplified):** Buyers are mainland users buying **foreign-region** cards: "美区/港区/土区" Apple, Steam and PSN cards, "Steam钱包充值码", "PSN港服点卡". Google Play is not usable in mainland China. **和平精英 does NOT accept PUBG Mobile UC** (it uses 点券). Content about "美区 Apple ID 充值 USDT" is a real niche. Competitors are Bitrefill zh-Hans pages and V2EX/CSDN threads.
8. **Portuguese (Brazil):** The vocabulary is "gift card" and "cartão presente". Sellers list fixed BRL values (KaBuM!: R$35, 60, 100, 250, 300, 530). Main competitors are KaBuM!, Mercado Livre, Nuuvem and dundle. Brazil-account (BRL) codes are mandatory on PSN.
9. **German and French:** "Guthaben" (de) and "carte / code" (fr) dominate. dundle, Startselect and mydealz deals own the head terms. The crypto long-tail is CoinGate's localized articles ("psn geschenkkarte mit krypto kaufen", "achetez une carte cadeau psn avec des cryptomonnaies"). They are beatable with USDT-specific pages.
10. **News hook for 2026:** Valve announced in June 2026 that it is **discontinuing physical Steam gift cards** at retail (no restocking; expected gone by end of 2026). Digital cards and existing wallet codes keep working. An evergreen "Are Steam gift cards discontinued? (Digital codes still work)" page in each language is a timely, low-competition opportunity.

## 12. Facts for landing pages (verified, with sources)

Each fact gives its source tier: **official** (brand help page) or **secondary** (reseller, press, forums).

### Steam Wallet codes: currency and region
- **Official redeem URL:** https://store.steampowered.com/account/redeemwalletcode. The page says gift cards and wallet codes "will be converted to the currency of your Steam Wallet upon redemption", "regardless of where they were purchased" (official).
- **In practice this does not hold.** Steam community threads from 2020 to 2024 consistently report that codes issued in a **different currency than the account** fail with "There was an error redeeming the entered code". Users describe it as a currency or region lock (for example, USD retail cards will not redeem on MYR accounts). Treat the official "converted" wording as unreliable for cross-currency codes. **Landing page copy: "Buy the code in your Steam Wallet currency."** (secondary: steamcommunity.com threads; SEAGM error guide)
- **Turkey, Argentina and the MENA-USD / LATAM-USD regions:** from 2023-11-20, Steam switched Turkey and Argentina from TRY/ARS to USD via two new regions, **MENA-USD** and **LATAM-USD** (25 countries in total). Existing wallet balances were converted to USD, and accounts in those regions can redeem **USD wallet codes** (official Steam FAQ, plus SteamDB and PC Gamer).
- **Physical cards discontinued (June 2026):** Valve said it will not restock physical Steam gift cards at retail and expects them gone by the end of 2026, citing scams. Digital gift cards and existing cards and wallet codes still work (secondary: PC Gamer, Tom's Hardware, Engadget, Bitdefender).
- Wallet funds cannot be withdrawn or transferred to another account (secondary: Bitrefill and GamerMarkt terms, consistent with the Steam Subscriber Agreement).

### PlayStation Store / PSN: region lock
- "The voucher code needs to be for the same region as your account." The account country/region **cannot be changed** after creation. The region is printed on the card (a flag on physical vouchers) (official: playstation.com support "Problems using voucher codes", "Check account country/region", "Redeem PS Store voucher code").
- **Redeem:** in a web browser, go to store.playstation.com, select your profile, choose Redeem Code and enter the 12-digit code. Or redeem on PS5/PS4 in the Store menu, or in the PlayStation App (official).
- Each code works once, and funds cannot move between accounts. For child accounts, wallet top-up codes must be redeemed on the family manager's account (official).
- **Contradicting common assumption:** a currency match is not enough. A Belgian EUR card does not work on a German EUR account, because PSN locks by **country**, not currency (official rule "same region as your account"; CoinGate makes the same point for EUR countries).

### Google Play gift cards: country restriction
- "Play gift cards may only be redeemed and used in the country and currency in which they were purchased." "The country or region listed in your Google Play account must match the country or region where the gift card" was purchased (official: support.google.com/googleplay/answer/3422734).
- Cards are geo-restricted. Error text: "Your Play balance can only be used in [country/region]". If you change your Play country, **the balance does not transfer** and stays usable in the original country (official: answer/11564359, answer/3422734).
- **Redeem URL:** https://play.google.com/redeem (official: answer/3422659). Codes are usually 16 characters.
- **Russia:** Google paused Play billing for users in Russia on 2022-03-10 (no app or in-app purchases). Do not market Google Play cards for Russian accounts (official: Google Play developer help 11950272; 9to5Google). Google Play is also not available in mainland China (common knowledge, not re-verified here).

### Apple Gift Card / App Store & iTunes: country restriction
- "You can't redeem Apple Gift Cards, App Store Cards, or App Store & iTunes Gift Cards outside of the country or region of purchase". Example: a card bought in France cannot be redeemed in the US App Store (official: support.apple.com/108285).
- Apple **Store** Gift Cards (for physical Apple Store purchases) are a different product and cannot be redeemed in the App Store or iTunes Store (official: 108285).
- **Redeem:** iPhone/iPad: App Store, account icon, Redeem Gift Card or Code. Mac: App Store, your name, Redeem Code. Windows: Apple Music or Apple TV app, your name, Redeem. Web: **apple.com/redeem**, linked from Apple's own redeem article. Codes start with "X" (official: support.apple.com/118242, 108285).
- Community answers add that the Apple Account's country (and usually a billing address in that country) must match the card (secondary: Apple Communities).

### Razer Gold: global vs regional PINs
- Official: a Razer Gold PIN can "reload Razer Gold wallets in the same region they were purchased from", or be used for **direct top-up** at participating game sites (merchant list: gold.razer.com/gold/catalog) (official: razer.com/eu-en/razer-gold-pin).
- **Redeem:** sign in at https://gold.razer.com, click Reload Now, choose Razer Gold PIN, enter the PIN and complete 2-step verification (official).
- **Global vs regional:** Razer wallets have a region. A **Global (USD) PIN** reloads a **Global** wallet. Razer Insider staff and user replies say a US PIN cannot be reloaded into a Global wallet and vice versa, and that Global USD PINs cannot reload various regional wallets. Support can sometimes change a wallet's region. Reports conflict on Global PINs into US wallets (secondary: insider.razer.com threads). **Landing page copy:** "Global PINs work with Razer Gold wallets set to the Global region and for direct top-up at supported games. Check your wallet region at gold.razer.com before buying."
- Validity: dundle and AR-PAY say non-European PINs expire 1 year after purchase. In Europe, PINs have no end date, but inactivity fees can apply after 12 months (secondary: dundle, CoinGate fr). Verify against Razer's terms before stating.

### PUBG Mobile UC: redemption and versions
- **Redeem codes:** at Midasbuy, log in, choose country/region, open the PUBG MOBILE Redeem page, enter the **Player ID** and the code, and confirm. URL per Midasbuy's own FAQ: **https://www.midasbuy.com/midasbuy/ot/redeem/pubgm** (short form midasbuy.com/ot/redeem/pubgm also used by resellers) (official: cdn.midasbuy.com FAQ). UC is credited to whatever Player ID is entered, so a wrong ID means UC goes to someone else and cannot be refunded (secondary: Lapakgaming, AR-PAY).
- **Redeem code vs top-up:** a "redeem code" is a voucher redeemed on Midasbuy by the buyer. A "top-up" / "ID ile yükleme" / "via ID" is the seller crediting UC directly to a Player ID. Both need only the Player ID, never a password (secondary: GamerMarkt, Buffget).
- **Global UC codes do NOT work on:** Japan/Korea servers, Taiwan, Vietnam (VNG), mainland China, and **BGMI** (India) (secondary but consistent: Buffget, SEAGM, Topuplist product terms).
- **BGMI (India)** is a separate Krafton India game with its own UC. Midasbuy does not sell BGMI UC, and Codashop is BGMI's officially recognized store. BGMI promo codes use BGMI's own redemption site (secondary: Sportskeeda, BGMI redeem-code coverage).
- **和平精英 (Game for Peace, mainland China)** uses **点券** (and 绿洲币 on iOS), not UC. It is a separate version, and recharge pages state it does not apply to PUBG MOBILE (secondary: Topuplist hpjy-cn, Moegirl wiki).
- **Turkey:** Turkish sellers sell separate **TR-ID** and **Global-ID** UC packs. TR-ID packs require an account on the Turkish server (secondary: GamerMarkt).

### Crypto payment context (for copy)
- None of Steam, Sony, Apple or Google accept crypto directly. Crypto is only the payment rail for buying the code (Steam dropped bitcoin in Dec 2017) (secondary: Bitrefill, CoinGate, CriptoNoticias).
- The USDT network must match what the wallet sends. Sending ERC20 USDT to a TRC20 payment request can fail or lose funds. This is a common FAQ to answer on every crypto page (secondary: CoinGate es, CoinsBee guide).

## 13. Contradictions with common assumptions (quick list)

1. **"Steam converts any code to your currency."** Steam's redeem page says so, but cross-currency codes routinely fail. Sell by account currency.
2. **"Turkish Steam needs TL codes."** False since 2023-11-20: Turkey is MENA-USD and uses USD codes.
3. **"Same currency is enough for PSN."** False: PSN locks by account country, not currency (EUR card from Belgium will not work on a German account).
4. **"Global" PUBG UC is truly global.** False: it excludes JP/KR, TW, VN, mainland China (和平精英) and BGMI.
5. **"Razer Gold Global works everywhere."** Not reliably. It reloads Global-region wallets and works for direct top-up. Regional wallets need regional PINs.
6. **"You can move Google Play balance when you move country."** False: the balance stays tied to the original country.
7. **"Steam gift cards are being discontinued."** Only physical retail cards (from 2026). Digital codes and wallet codes continue.
